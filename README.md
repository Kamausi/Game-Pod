# Game-Pod

## Comet Hop

A one-tap arcade game. Open `index.html` in any browser.

- **Tap / click / Space** to launch your comet off its orbit.
- Fly into the next planet's orbit to get captured. Leave the screen and you drift off.
- Aim through the middle of an orbit for a **PERFECT** and build a combo (up to x5 points).
- Every 5 perfects in a row triggers **SUPERNOVA**: double stardust, a stardust magnet, and faster music.
- **Power-ups:** Shield (saves you once), Magnet, 2X Dust, Slow-Mo.
- **Special planets:** golden planets pay a +5 jackpot; nebula planets carry a ring of stardust.
- **Missions:** three at a time, each pays stardust and comes back harder.
- **Daily gift** grows with your day streak.
- Lifetime stardust unlocks ten comets automatically.
- **M** mutes sound.
